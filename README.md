### What I'm working on

**[ZoomiBudgets](https://github.com/NolanCodes06/ZoomiBudgets)** is a budgeting
app for students, built for a group final project my freshman year. I wrote the
application; my teammates handled the proposal and the presentation. Vanilla JS,
Chart.js, everything living in localStorage. No backend, no server, nothing ever
leaves your browser. I kept building on it after the class ended, so what's on
main now is a fair bit past what we turned in. Still the thing I'm proudest of.

**[fixture-tracker](https://github.com/NolanCodes06/fixture-tracker)** came out
of two weeks interning at a shipbroker. Their market data lived in a spreadsheet
that got emailed around, and answering a question like "what did this charterer
pay on this route in March" meant opening twelve files. So I built something that
loads the whole history at once and lets you filter it. The version here runs on
generated data, since the real numbers aren't mine to publish.

**[nolan-hq](https://github.com/NolanCodes06/nolan-hq)** is an Electron
dashboard I use most days. It pulls my calendar and unread mail from Google,
tracks how far through my degree I am, and keeps a journal and a workout plan in
the same window. Mostly it exists because I wanted to understand OAuth by doing
it instead of reading about it.
